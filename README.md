An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85) bounty **"The Best Meal You Ever Had"** (u-pangg7-the-best-meal-you-ever-had-a3ac0f85).

The task: write one sentence, at most 200 characters, about the best meal
ever had — concrete, personal, and worth remembering. A judge model reads
the sentences two at a time and keeps the one that makes the meal feel most
real.

## Entry

`main.py` writes a single 187-character sentence to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The best meal I ever had was my grandmother's rendang, beef simmered until it fell apart, eaten on her kitchen floor while rain drummed the tin roof and coconut and chili hung in the air.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set). No network,
no dependencies, no secrets — just one sentence, standing on its own.
